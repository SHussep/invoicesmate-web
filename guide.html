The Invoice Mate guide

From first invoice
to organised records.

A practical walkthrough for your working day. Start with your business details, then follow the steps below.

01 · Set up02 · Invoice03 · Expenses04 · Reports
01 / YOUR BUSINESS

Start with the details you use every day.

Open More and your business profile to review your business name, contact details and ABN. In settings, check your invoice numbering, payment terms and GST registration setting. Add the payment details you want customers to see.

Use the settings that apply to your business. If you are unsure about GST registration or tax treatment, confirm them with your registered tax or BAS agent.

02 / INVOICES

Create. Preview. Share.

  1. Choose a customer

    Open Invoices and tap the add button. Select a saved customer or add their details.

  2. Add the work

    Enter a description, quantity and price for each item. Check the dates, tax treatment, discounts and payment terms.

  3. Preview your document

    Open the PDF preview. Choose a template and accent colour, and check the business details, line items and total.

  4. Share and track

    Share the PDF using the available share options. Return to the invoice to review its status and record payments as they arrive.

03 / EXPENSES

Keep the receipt with the record.

  1. Add an expense

    Open Expenses and tap the add button. Enter the merchant, date and amount.

  2. Add the context

    Choose a category, check the GST amount and write a note that will help you recognise the expense later.

  3. Attach supporting details

    Add a receipt photo where available, then save. Check your entries regularly so your reports reflect your records.

04 / REPORTS & EXPORTS

Bring the whole year together.

  1. Review your reports

    Open More and Reports. Select the period you want to review and check income, expenses and outstanding amounts.

  2. Check before exporting

    Review missing details and unusual entries. Reports and GST estimates depend on the accuracy of your records.

  3. Choose your financial year

    In Expenses, select the financial year and tap the share icon at the top right. Choose the record types and the supported PDF or Excel format.

  4. Share with your accountant

    Use the export sharing options to save or send the file yourself. Your accountant can review the records and advise on the next steps.
